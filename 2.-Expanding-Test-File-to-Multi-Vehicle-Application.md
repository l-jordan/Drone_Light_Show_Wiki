The initial version of expanding to multi-drone simulation utilises just a single node, acting on behalf of the entire simulation. This encompasses all of the drones as well as their follower-leader logic. In this case, the path planning is performed on the simulation level, such that the drones will only know their target location in their own coordinate frame. This can be done in a straightforward manner as the relevant topics of all simulated drones are known to the node and the coordinates and their transformations can be performed as a single-loop iteration where the drone name (px4-1, px4-2 or px4-3) is varied in front of the appropriate uORB message.
