Welcome to the drone_light_show wiki! Here you will find how Hubert and Lauren built their simulation. For this Capita Selecta project, we are building a drone light show using three autonomously driven drones running PX4 Autopilot software. We will do this using Gazebo Ignition as our simulation environment and ROS2 as our autonomous communicator between the drone and the companion computer (how it would be done in reality. This wiki will bring you step-by-step through our procedure to create this simulation.


We first ask that you look at the README.md file so you can get a general idea of how the communication between the drone and ROS2 works and how to set up the environment.
